language: de
language-anchors: Meeting, Standup, Agenda
forbidden: Besprechung, Tagesordnung
must-change: das meeting->Das Meeting; standup->Standup; agenda->Agenda; auf nachmittag->auf den Nachmittag; schick->schicke
tone-anchors: morgen früh
min-length-ratio: 0.9
max-length-ratio: 1.3
why: Three anglicisms that are ordinary German office vocabulary. Rule 8 keeps
  them; a model that translates them has misread it as a purity exercise.
  "nachmittag" is a noun and needs its article and capital — an earlier version
  of this reference wrote "auf nachmittags", which is not standard German and
  changes the meaning to "to afternoons".
